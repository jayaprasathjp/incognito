# 🏆 INCØGNITØ Player Handbook
Welcome to **INCØGNITØ** — the ultimate competitive platform for the Nigerian university esports community. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to compete, win, and claim your glory.

---

### 1. Getting Started
To join the competition, you first need to create your profile:
*   **Email:** Your primary account identifier and login method.
*   **Institution:** Select your Nigerian university from the list.
*   **WhatsApp:** Used for direct contact if there are match issues.
*   **Referrals:** If a friend invited you, enter their code during signup to help them earn rewards.

---

### 2. Joining a Tournament
Once you're logged in, check your **Dashboard** for active tournaments.
1.  **Enter your Alias:** Choose your public in-game name. This alias must be **globally unique**—if someone else on the platform is already using it, you will need to choose another! You can, however, use your own alias in future tournaments.
2.  **Choose Your Session:** Pick your preferred playing time:
    *   ☀️ **Morning:** 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM
    *   🌤️ **Afternoon:** 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
    *   🌙 **Evening:** 5:30 PM – 8:30 PM
3.  **Payment:** Review the entry fee and pay securely via **Flutterwave**. Once confirmed, you'll see a "You're In!" badge on your dashboard.

---

### 3. The Match Day
When the tournament begins, your matches will appear on your **Dashboard**. 

#### Step 1: Check-in
*   The **"I'M READY"** button appears **15 minutes before** your scheduled match time.
*   **Important:** You must check in within 30 minutes of the match time. If you don't, your opponent can claim a **Walkover Win**, and you will be disqualified.

#### Step 2: The Room Code
*   Once both players are ready, the match goes live.
*   **Home Player (Player 1):** You are responsible for creating the game room and sharing the code in the app.
*   **Away Player (Player 2):** You will see the code on your screen as soon as it's shared.

---

### 4. Reporting Scores & Proof
After your FIFA/eFootball match is over, both players must report the scores immediately.

1.  **Enter Score:** Input how many goals you scored and how many your opponent scored.
2.  **Upload Proof:** You **MUST** upload a clear screenshot of the final match result screen. This is your only protection if a dispute occurs.
3.  **Auto-Resolution:** If both players agree on the score, the match is finalized instantly, and the winner moves to the next round.

---

### 5. Disputes & Issues
If things don't go as planned, the system has built-in protections:

*   **Conflict:** If you and your opponent report different scores, the match goes to **Admin Review**. An admin will check both screenshots to decide the winner.
*   **No Response:** If you submit your score and your opponent does nothing, they have **1 hour** to respond. If they remain silent, you **automatically win** (3-0).
*   **No Room Code:** If the Home Player fails to share a room code within the hour, the Away Player is awarded the win.

---

### 6. Advancing & Winning
*   **The Bracket:** You can view the full tournament progress on the **Tournament Details** page.
*   **Leaderboard:** Track your points and rankings. Every win earns you **3 points**.
*   **Crowning the Champion:** The winner of the final match is crowned the champion.
*   **Prizes:** If you win, you'll see an alert to enter your **Bank Details**. Fill these out immediately so your prize money can be transferred!

---

### 💡 Pro-Tips for Players
*   **Stay Notified:** Keep an eye on your dashboard for round updates.
*   **Screen Cap Everything:** Capture screenshots of everything — room codes, final scores, and even connection errors.
*   **Be On Time:** Respect the schedule. A no-show is an automatic loss.

**Good luck, and see you on the pitch! ⚽**
